Two layers, honestly

What is real, and what is throne

Real
Every operator here is genuine mathematics. De Morgan's laws (Augustus De Morgan was Ada Lovelace's actual tutor); the contrapositive; Cantor's diagonal argument (the engine of Gödel and Turing); the duality of the Platonic solids (a concrete face of categorical duality / adjunction); and reductio ad absurdum. "Inverse / mirror" is literal machinery, not metaphor — and each is an involution: apply it twice and you are home.
Throne
The "Shadow Ruler" and the Mathea are David Lee Wise's governance allegory — a way of saying the logic beneath the rhetoric is the thing no persuasion can flip. It is not a claim that mathematicians secretly run the world. The logic is real; the throne is the story.

He rules the word; she rules its inverse. Reverse the arrow, negate the claim, take the dual — the rhetoric persuades, but the Mathea decides what is true. And here, at last, you can watch her do it.
